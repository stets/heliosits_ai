Claude Implementation for Small Businesses

Turn a Claude subscription into a useful business workflow.

Request a free discovery call

Start with a task your team already does

Claude can help draft, summarize, and organize information. Getting reliable value from it takes more than buying a subscription. Your team needs useful context, clear instructions, access to the right information, and a way to check the output.

Helios AI helps small businesses set up Claude around their actual work. We can configure a shared approach for a team, connect approved tools, or build a custom integration when a manual process needs to run repeatedly.

What implementation can include

  • A review of the task, the systems it touches, and how you'll measure improvement.
  • Claude account and workspace setup, reusable instructions, and relevant business context.
  • Connections to your existing tools where supported, or a scoped API integration.
  • A tested pilot with sample inputs, documented limitations, and human review steps.
  • Team training, handover documentation, and a discussion of ongoing support.

Examples of a first workflow

Customer support: Draft a reply using approved support material, with a person reviewing it before sending.

Document review: Extract agreed fields from recurring documents and flag incomplete or uncertain records for review.

Sales operations: Turn meeting notes into a structured follow-up draft and CRM update for approval.

These are examples of possible projects. During discovery, we check the tools, permissions, and data needed for your particular workflow.

A practical rollout

We start by mapping one process and choosing success criteria, such as time spent per task or the number of records requiring correction. Then we build a small pilot, test ordinary and difficult inputs, and train your team before expanding it. Your proposal sets out the deliverables, pricing, and timeline.

When a different approach is better

A fixed rule or standard software integration may be a better choice for predictable work. We also look at what happens when the AI output is wrong. Decisions involving payments, customer commitments, or important business records need appropriate checks and approvals.

Claude implementation questions

Can you work with our existing software?

We review your tools and the connectors or APIs available. Some projects need a custom connection; others work well with approved exports. We'll establish that before quoting implementation.

Do you provide Claude subscriptions?

The business should own its software accounts and subscriptions. We help with setup and discuss subscription and usage costs separately from consulting.

Can you train our team without building custom software?

Yes. Claude team training can focus on existing tools, reusable instructions, and safe review habits. If a task needs to run automatically, explore workflow automation.

What would you like to take off your team's plate?

Tell us about one repetitive task and the tools you use. We'll follow up by email to discuss whether AI is a good fit and arrange a free discovery call. No commitment required.
