Claude Training for Small Business Teams

Help your team use Claude on real work.

Request a free discovery call

Training built around your team's tasks

Your team doesn't need another general AI presentation. It needs practice with the documents, questions, and recurring tasks it actually handles. Helios AI provides hands-on Claude training for small businesses that want a consistent way to use AI at work.

We start by learning what your team does, what tools it uses, and what information it can share. Then we shape the session around useful exercises and review habits.

What your team can learn

  • How to explain a task with the context, constraints, and output format Claude needs.
  • How to turn a useful prompt into reusable instructions for recurring work.
  • How to work with documents and reference material while respecting business data policies.
  • How to check facts, calculations, missing information, and uncertain answers.
  • How to decide when a task needs human judgment or a different tool.

Practical exercises

Operations: Turn a procedure into a checklist, then review it for missing steps.

Customer service: Draft a reply using an approved policy and check it for unsupported promises.

Sales and administration: Organize meeting notes into next actions, then verify names, dates, and commitments.

We choose exercises with you and use approved or sanitized examples. These are possible topics; the final training plan depends on your roles and goals.

What a training engagement can include

A discovery discussion establishes participants, experience levels, tools, and priorities. A tailored workshop gives people time to practice and ask questions. Depending on the agreed scope, the handover can include reusable prompt examples, a short reference guide, and recommendations for the next workflow to improve.

If training exposes a process that would benefit from integration, we can separately scope Claude implementation or workflow automation. You can also keep the engagement focused entirely on training.

Use AI with clear boundaries

We help teams recognize what to review and when to stop. That includes following company policies for confidential information, checking important claims, and keeping a person responsible for customer-facing output. Training should make judgment more consistent, alongside making people faster.

Claude training questions

Does everyone need technical experience?

No. We adapt exercises to the people attending and their day-to-day work. Basic team training can focus on Claude's interface and practical review skills without writing code.

Can you train different departments together?

We can plan shared foundations and role-specific exercises. Tell us the team size, roles, and experience levels so we can propose an appropriate format.

How do you price training?

We quote based on preparation, participants, session scope, and any follow-up material or support. Software subscriptions are separate. Request a discovery call to discuss your team.

What would you like to take off your team's plate?

Tell us about one repetitive task and the tools you use. We'll follow up by email to discuss whether AI is a good fit and arrange a free discovery call. No commitment required.
