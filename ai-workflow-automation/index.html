Automate a process with a clear result

Copying information from emails into a CRM, sorting incoming documents, and preparing routine reports can consume hours of attention. AI workflow automation connects the steps and uses AI where the input needs interpretation.

Helios AI helps small businesses map these processes, build the connections, and test the result. The goal is a workflow your team can understand and operate, with a clear way to catch mistakes.

Tasks we can help you automate

  • Document intake: Extract fields from recurring PDFs or forms, validate required information, and route exceptions to a person.
  • CRM updates: Turn approved emails or notes into structured records and follow-up drafts.
  • Support triage: Categorize incoming requests and prepare answers using approved reference material.
  • Recurring reporting: Gather information from agreed sources and prepare a summary for review.

These are starting points, not promises that every tool supports every integration. We review API access, permissions, data quality, and volume before proposing the build.

From manual steps to a working system

  1. Map the current process. Identify the trigger, inputs, decisions, output, and person responsible for it.
  2. Choose the right tools. Use ordinary automation for predictable rules and AI for tasks that benefit from interpretation.
  3. Build and test a pilot. Try representative inputs, missing data, duplicates, and service failures.
  4. Hand over the workflow. Document how to review results, handle exceptions, and pause the process.

Build in checks before scaling

A useful automation needs more than a successful demo. We consider permissions, duplicate prevention, validation, review steps, and what happens when a connected service is unavailable. The exact safeguards depend on the consequences of a mistake.

We agree on how to assess the pilot against your existing process. That might mean comparing processing time, corrections, or manual handoffs. We don't promise a savings percentage before understanding your task.

Workflow automation questions

Do we need to replace our current tools?

Often the best starting point is the tools you already use. We check whether their APIs, connectors, or exports can support the process before recommending changes.

Will AI send messages or change records automatically?

That depends on the agreed design. A pilot can prepare drafts and proposed updates for human approval. We discuss permissions and consequences before enabling automatic actions.

What should we bring to a discovery call?

Bring one recurring task, the tools involved, an estimate of how often it happens, and an example with private information removed. If you mainly need help using Claude, see Claude implementation or team training.

What would you like to take off your team's plate?

Tell us about one repetitive task and the tools you use. We'll follow up by email to discuss whether AI is a good fit and arrange a free discovery call. No commitment required.
